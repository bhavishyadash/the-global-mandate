# Session 102: reviewed fiscal decisions

## Delivered

- Main income, consumption and corporate tax controls now open a confirmation review before applying a change. The review shows current and proposed rates, modeled annual revenue, national approval and group approval using the existing fictional preview model.
- Total and category spending controls also require review. They show the current and proposed annual amount, modeled annual balance and daily flow. Total spending warns that it resets category shares.
- Reviews pause the main clock and retain the proposed arguments. A changed country snapshot invalidates the review; cancelling does not change the world. Successful decisions update the visible fiscal controls and synchronize the selected World Lab tax value in shared mode.
- These are prototype direct decisions, not realistic US legislative passage or calibrated economic forecasts. World Lab's separate experimental controls remain separate.

## Validation and limitations

- Automated UI checks exercise the actual income-tax review button, cancellation, stale-state rejection, successful income and corporate tax changes, total and category spending changes, and invalid spending proposals.
- The simulations and historical values remain fictional. Legal authority, wider economic effects, realistic budget procedures, and director playtesting remain upcoming.
