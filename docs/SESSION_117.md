# Session 117: Editable finance and tax law controls

## Target
Connect finance and tax catalogue entries to the existing reviewed and state-checked tax and spending decisions, including live deficit previews. Preserve existing law prototypes and label unavailable topics honestly. Related to #169.

## Completed
- Added four clearly labeled editable prototypes to the Laws catalogue: flat income, corporate and consumption tax rates, and budget-category spending. The existing healthcare and procurement controls remain available.
- Selecting an editable row reveals controls, a live deficit/approval or budget preview and a review button. Confirmation uses the existing snapshot-checked decision system; preview alone does not change the simulation.
- Search, category navigation and selected-row preservation work across refreshes. Invalid amounts are disabled. The catalogue validates the fixed handler list and remains packaged offline.

## Limitations
- These are simplified fictional flat-rate and spending prototypes, not real US tax brackets or a model of constitutional authority. They apply through the existing immediate decision path, not through a fully modeled legislative process.
- Most catalogue entries remain planned and cannot be edited or enacted. Implementing the requested breadth of laws, choices, legal procedure and effects remains open.
