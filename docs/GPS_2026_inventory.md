# GPS 2026: initial decision and reaction inventory

This is a partial, read-only survey of the installed Geo-Political Simulator 2026 Edition. Source references are PDF page numbers in the installed `user_manual_EN.pdf` (game manual, GM) and `manuel_modding_tool_EN.pdf` (mod manual, MM). Descriptions are paraphrased. Documented effects are what the manual says, not independently tested behavior. Proposed reactions are ideas for our own game, not claims about GPS's formulas. The manuals do not enumerate every law or decision.

## Modding capabilities documented in the installation

The mod manual describes editing national data, cities, regions, political parties, laws, taxation, armies, bases, organizations and companies; changing international relations and borders; planning conditional events; and customizing character names and portraits. It does not itself provide a complete list of the base game's laws or the simulation's equations. The event editor describes triggers and outcomes including relations, character opinions, unrest, conflicts and elections. (MM 2–4, 6, 21–29; GM 64.) An editor capability is not automatically a normal in-game player decision.

## File inspection and limitations

- Installation inspected: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Geo-Political Simulator 2026 Edition`.
- English player and modding PDFs were readable and provided the evidence above.
- The installation contains numerous `.gp`, `.dat`, `.dad` and `.gt` files. Their contents and formats have not been decoded in this survey; their extensions and names alone do not establish what they contain.
- The `.gui` files sampled are readable UTF-16-like interface/layout definitions with control identifiers and coordinates. The sampled file did not establish a complete law list or simulation equations.
- There are text, audio and video files; their mere presence does not establish their role in decision logic. Files were counted by extension, not individually analyzed.
- Executables and binary assets were not decompiled, unpacked or modified. No controlled in-game experiments were performed.
- Therefore exact formulas, numerical coefficients, every country-specific choice, prerequisites, all party/leader reactions, and a complete list of GPS laws remain unknown. Manual descriptions can be simplified or out of date relative to the running build.
