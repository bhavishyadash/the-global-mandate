# Session 43: agreement revenue and fiscal transparency

Each active cooperation agreement now adds a fictional $20 million annual partner contribution, alongside its $10 million annual cost. The contribution is separate from base revenue and tax bases, accrues daily while active, and stops on expiry or termination. It is included in the actual fiscal ledger, treasury, spending and tax previews, and simulated forecasts. The dashboard shows both contribution and cost. Existing version-11 saves with active agreements acquire this new effect upon loading; past ledger entries are not rewritten. Tests cover the revenue breakdown, preview, daily collection, expiry and forecast consistency. These are arbitrary scenario values, not economic, trade, aid or diplomatic predictions.
