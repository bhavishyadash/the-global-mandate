# Session 28 — repeal the test policy

Related to issues #22 and #25. Add a legislative vote to repeal the fictional procurement policy, cancel its implementation or recurring cost and permit a later new proposal. Record outcomes and verify approval thresholds, accounting and saves. Broader legislative and agency systems remain open.

## Result

The UI shows projected repeal support and offers a vote. In this simple model parties opposing the policy vote to repeal it, and more than 50 of 100 seats are needed. A successful vote clears the policy and implementation progress, ending the recurring cost; a failed vote changes nothing. A new proposal remains possible if it later gains majority support. Local tests cover failed and successful votes, repeal during implementation and after completion, cost removal and save/load.
